The test

30-minute threshold time trial

Before
  • Two easy or rest days first; no long run in the previous 48 h; normal sleep.
  • Cool and calm: ≤ 25 °C. In Delhi that means pre-dawn from October to March, otherwise an AC treadmill at 1% incline.
  • Skip days with AQI above about 200.
  • Flat track or loop with no traffic lights. Chest strap if you have one; otherwise wear the watch snug.
During
  1. 15 min warm-up: 10 min easy, 3 × 20 s strides, 2 min easy.
  2. 30 min solo at the best even effort you can hold (10-15 km race effort). Don't sprint the start.
  3. Press LAP at exactly 10:00 of the 30 minutes. Never pause the watch; a stop over 15 s spoils the test.
  4. 10 min easy cool-down.
After
  • Sync COROS, then analyse the run below.
  • Threshold HR = average HR of minutes 10-30, after removing pauses, dropouts, spikes and cadence lock.
  • Not a group run or a race: both raise heart rate.
  • Read the evidence in docs/HR_ZONES_RESEARCH.md.
